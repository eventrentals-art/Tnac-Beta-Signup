About TNAC

We didn't build TNAC because fitness needed another app. We built it because fitness needed a better way.

Fitness should feel like progress, not a chore.

Nathan & Adam
Brothers and founders of TNAC

Founded by brothers Nathan and Adam, TNAC was born from a simple realization: there were countless fitness apps, but none brought everything together in one place that was easy to use, motivating, and genuinely rewarding.

Read our story

After four years of working out, Nathan and Adam knew the struggle firsthand. The excitement of early gains eventually faded, and staying consistent wasn't always easy. Having each other as workout partners made showing up easier and the journey more rewarding.

After interviewing hundreds of people, they discovered that many shared the same struggles: staying consistent, finding community, and feeling like their hard work was paying off.

That's why they built TNAC.

TNAC brings fitness, guidance, AI-powered tools, community, and gamified progress together in one place. Whether someone is just starting out or has been training for years, TNAC is designed to make fitness simpler, more engaging, and more rewarding.

The goal is bigger than completing workouts. It's helping people build habits, celebrate their progress, and become healthier, more confident versions of themselves.

Because fitness isn't just about getting stronger. It's about discovering what you're capable of.
